# SkinPlan v17.4 — scientific audit / safety refinement

Changes:
- X3 Levels remain the device priority input, but wording now states explicitly that X3 is not used as a clinical diagnosis.
- Spot-heat can no longer create a pigmentation treatment by itself.
- Pore score can no longer create a BHA/congestion treatment by itself.
- Collagen or texture score can no longer create a retinoid/ageing treatment by itself; wrinkle is the primary ageing trigger.
- Barrier-recovery phase is now a minimum 2 weeks and criterion-based; it continues until skin is comfortable for at least 7 days.
- Sensitive profiles are capped at 3 PM treatment nights/week; other profiles at 4.
- Added safety checkbox for patients already using retinoid / benzoyl peroxide / exfoliating acid.
- Every treatment protocol now states that the automatic frequency is a conservative starter schedule and escalation must not exceed the current manufacturer label.
- Sunscreen instructions now include reapplication during sustained outdoor exposure and after water/sweating/towel drying.
- Acne language now distinguishes a device acne signal from clinically diagnosed acne and strengthens referral wording.
- Algorithm page documents the evidence boundary between cosmetic skincare support and medical acne/photoaging treatment.
